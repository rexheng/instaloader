## Notes behind the scores

- **Hook 8.5.** The first frame is the puzzle (dots raining into two maps with live 537 counters). "Which one is chance?" lands at 0:09 with a 2.0 s hold, and the landing re-sees the opening exactly (3:12–3:21). The dot-pattern "which is random" demo is familiar to many viewers. Tension sags in the 1:38–2:05 algebra stretch.
- **Clarity 8.** It is concrete-first throughout and every number checks. Deductions: the false intermediate equation (1:45.5–1:49), the asserted e^−λ limit (1:41), "choose k" with no picture (1:46), and the 39-vs-39.3 match left implicit (2:13).
- **Visual 7.5.** The palette is semantic and stable (blue = observed, purple = chance, red = flagged/aim, yellow = focus), with a clear hierarchy, safe margins and readable type. Several minor issues go beyond the 8-anchor allowance: the header overlap (3:20), "1 targets" (0:36), the corner-pinned formula over an empty half-frame (1:38–2:01), the unlabelled table rows (2:43–3:11), the halo colour inconsistency (0:59), and the visually thin Clarke reveal (2:29–2:41).
- **Motion 8.** Persistence is genuinely good: the grid sweep, lagged outlines with a counter, TransformFromCopy of 1/576, cells flying into bars, and B's bars morphing from A's. The shredded morph (1:53.5) and the P(0)→P(k) crossfade ghosting ("P(⁴)", 1:44.5–1:45.5) are the two non-meaningful moves.
- **Audio 8.** Pace (158 wpm), loudness and true peak are on target, and deictics land: "like this one" at 0:35 is on the red square, "these purple bars" at 2:03–2:05 on the growing bars, "537 over 576" at 2:35–2:39 on the flagged fraction. "Poisson" is pronounced correctly. The cadence is metronomic.
- **Concision 8.5.** There is almost no slack. At 3:22 it runs long against the 3:00 template, and the payoff number read (2:41–2:52) could be tighter.
- **Originality 8.** The detector framing, the "too even is the fingerprint of a rule" inversion and the honest whole-county failure are a fresh angle on a classic example. No slop phrases.
