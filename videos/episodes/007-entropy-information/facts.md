This sheet lists every number spoken or shown, how it was checked, and where it comes from.
Recompute the corpus numbers with `python3 data.py <brown dir>`, which writes `data.json`; the scene reads that file. The guess-number entropy (1.42) is computed in `scene.py` from the record in `data.json`.

- 1.42 is computed from one 102-symbol passage. The narration says "about one point four" and presents it as an upper estimate. Shannon's 0.6–1.3 comes from his larger N = 100 experiment.
