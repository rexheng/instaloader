EPISODE: 075 — "Mild Preferences, Segregated Towns"
IDEA: In Schelling's model, households that demand only a third of matching neighbors still sort a random town to about three-quarters matching, because each departure tips a neighbor who was content (and at a third the starting town has two and a half times as many unhappy households as at a quarter), so a segregated map alone cannot reveal what its residents wanted.
