# Episode 20 — A Dinosaur Hiding in Your Summary Statistics

**One idea:** two means, two standard deviations and a correlation pin down exactly one ellipse
(a centre, a box, and where the ellipse touches the box: on the best-fit line), so any shape with
that ellipse (a dinosaur, a star, Anscombe's four datasets) has the same five numbers and even the
same best-fit line. Only a plot shows the shape.

Runtime RUNTIME · WORDS words (budget ≤ 500, PRODUCTION_GUIDE §13) · narrator af_heart @ 0.84,
`CADENCE = "shaped"` (B20 and B21 at 0.82, so the letter names are not rushed).

## Palette (colour → one meaning, whole episode)

| colour | meaning |
|---|---|
| YELLOW | the summary: the dinosaur's five numbers and the geometry they pin down (centre, mean ± SD error bars, SD box, ellipse, touch dots, best-fit line, the bar graph built from ȳ and s_y). The B9 "what if" at r = 0.80 (ellipse, touch dots, run, rise, dashed best-fit line, vertical slices and their midpoints) is the same summary of a hypothetical cloud, so it is drawn at 60 % and returns to full strength only when r is back at the dinosaur's −0.06; its live "r = …" label; Anscombe's five numbers, his summary geometry and the slope formula terms in the payoff; the dinosaur's values substituted into the slope at the landing |
| BLUE | actual data points (whatever dataset is on screen) |
| RED | a summary that has drifted away from the dinosaur's (live values that no longer match, the star's own drifting centre/error bars/box/ellipse, the missed median); the drifted cross turns yellow as it slides back onto the dinosaur's, so it is never red over a matching readout |
| GREEN | "matches" marks (always paired with the = glyph; RED always paired with ≠) |
| GRAY | axes and ticks, the cold-open "?" and the three candidate lines, the viewer's guess cloud and its dashed line, the simulated r = 0.80 cloud and its slice band (B9, tagged "simulated"), the two ghost ellipses, the target star and circle outlines. Grey TEXT (tick numbers, axis names, "your guess", "your line", headers, citations, the sourced percentages, the decimals note) uses a lighter grey, #A6ADB7 (8.0 : 1 on the background) |
| INK | live "now" values while they still match, words, the white that singles out points (the simulated slice's points in B9; the rings round Anscombe's outlier and lever point) |

Never red vs green alone: every RED/GREEN mark is also a ≠ / = glyph.

**B8.** The two standard deviations reach one deviation out on each side, and draw a box.
> s_x and s_y copies fly (one after the other, each source at 40 % while its copy leaves) to the
> half-arms they measure; the arms light up; the walls grow out of the arm tips on "box".
