# Episode 6 — facts and verification

Every number below is recomputed by `sim.py` (exact rational arithmetic, no sampling) or
`deck_pick.py` (seeded example decks), both in this directory. "GSR" = the
Gilbert–Shannon–Reeds model of a riffle shuffle. n = 52 throughout. Round 1 critics recomputed
every number independently (exact + Monte Carlo) and found no errors; items marked **(R1)** are
new or changed in the round-1 revision.
