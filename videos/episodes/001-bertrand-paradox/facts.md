## Things deliberately not claimed

- No claim about what "most people" guess (no survey data found).
- 1/2 is not presented as the universally correct answer: the episode states Jaynes's condition (unskilled tosses, circle small compared with the toss spread) and the limit (skill or a different mechanism gives a different answer).
- No claim that Jaynes's experiment "proved" 1/2 beyond what the paper says (128 successful tosses, ten length classes, low χ²). Their raw data were not published, so none is drawn.
- Not claimed that the class-size paradox is "Bertrand's paradox"; the narration says surveys hide "the same two words" (at random): picking a class vs picking a student is a choice of what is spread evenly, as with the chords.
