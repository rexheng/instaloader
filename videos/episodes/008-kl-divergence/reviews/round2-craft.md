## Notes behind the scores

- **Hook 8.** The data is on the first frame (0:00.5) and the prediction question arrives at 0:11.8, followed by a 2.0 s hold, so the hook lands in time. The landing answers it exactly. But the viewer is asked to bet on undefined notation, and the title gives the answer away (fix 7).
- **Clarity 8.** Concrete comes before abstract: one length, then every length, then the weight, then the integral. Every number checks out to three digits, and the limit is honest and demonstrated. Three things lose a careful viewer: the grey negative contributions are never explained, the 15 % is measured against A while the curve is labelled "posterior", and the shift/stretch demo shows a near-invisible change.
- **Visual 8.** The palette is semantic and held for the whole episode, and every hue sticks to its meaning. Type sizes pass §5: citations have about 26 px cap height and two-panel ticks about 28 px. Deductions: occluded glyphs (cap), the misdirected focal point at 2:30–2:36, the dead bottom-right quadrant from 1:48 to 2:28, the cluttered bracket frame at 2:53, the ambiguous same-baseline axis labels, and mixed A/B typography.
- **Motion 8.** Objects persist throughout and most moves carry meaning: the argument arcs, bar pull-outs, stacking, descent path and bracket-to-fraction flight. Deductions: the near-zero shift/stretch excursion, two layout changes that pass objects through each other, and the "judges" flash at 1:55, which reads as two disconnected yellow ticks.
- **Audio 8.** Pace is 160 wpm, loudness is on target, and pauses vary as round 1 asked. Deictics land within ±1 s ("sits here" 0:05, "18 and a half doublings" 1:35, "short ones like this" with the 36 % box at 1:39, "in the gap" with the boxes at 2:10). The exceptions are the ML sentence at 2:30, whose referent is not the focus, and the possibly swallowed "With" at 0:29.
- **Concision 8.** About 5 % could go: "It sits at the data's own mean and spread" repeats the pinned label, and "Both orders are in daily use" is soft.
- **Originality 8.** The example and data are fresh, and the "bill" and "doublings" framings are distinctive. The core picture is still the standard textbook mode-covering vs mode-seeking figure.
