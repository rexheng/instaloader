YouTube upload kit

Everything you need to post the videos: a download link for each one, plus a title, description and tags you can copy straight into YouTube Studio.

How to upload one video

  1. Click a download link below. The video saves with a clear file name.
  2. Go to studio.youtube.com, click Create, then Upload videos, and pick the file.
  3. Press Copy next to the title and paste it. Do the same for the description.
  4. Click Show more and paste the tags into the Tags box.
  5. Answer the two questions as shown in the settings box, then choose Public, Unlisted or Private.
  • Audience: "No, it's not made for kids". These are for a general audience.
  • Altered content: "No". The videos are animations with a generic computer voice. They don't show real people or real events that didn't happen.
  • Tip: Ep 1 is the only video that passed the 9/10 review. Upload the others as Unlisted first if you want to watch them before going public.
